About my work

I use R to explore data, build statistical models and communicate findings. My interests span scientific and social questions, from agricultural experiments and health outcomes to exoplanet clustering.

These projects were developed through statistics coursework at the University of Toronto. The original analytical code, calculations and saved results are preserved, with descriptive titles and project introductions added for this portfolio. Reports include data preparation, visualizations, statistical reasoning and interpretation; available R Markdown and Quarto files accompany the analyses.
